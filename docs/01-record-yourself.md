# 1. Record yourself

The model can only learn what is in your footage. Twenty careful minutes here matter more than any setting later.

## Set up the shot

- **Backdrop:** one plain colour, mid-grey if you can, flat and wrinkle-free. A grey bedsheet pulled tight works.
  Avoid green: it spills green light onto your skin.
- **Stand 1.5 metres (5 feet) in front of the backdrop** so you do not cast a shadow on it.
- **Light:** two lamps or a window in front of you, one each side, so your face has no hard shadow. Light the backdrop evenly.
  No light behind you.
- **Camera:** phone on a tripod or a shelf, at eye level, held **upright (portrait)**. Do not hold it in your hand.
- **Framing:** from your waist to a little above your head, with backdrop visible on **both sides of your arms**.
  Nothing else in the picture.
- **Wardrobe:** one plain top with no logo or pattern. Choose a colour clearly different from the backdrop
  (dark top on a grey backdrop). Wear the same thing for the whole recording.
- **Sound:** a quiet room. A clip-on microphone is better than the phone's own. No music, fan or TV.
- **iPhone:** in Settings → Camera → Record Video, turn **HDR Video off**. Use 1080p at 30 fps.

## What to record

- **25 to 30 minutes of you talking to the camera,** in a few takes. That gives 35 to 40 clips of 15 seconds.
  15 clips is the least the kit accepts; more is better.
- **Talk the way you want the clone to talk.** Same energy, same pace, your normal gestures. It will copy your habits.
- **Keep talking.** Tell stories, explain your work, read articles aloud in your own voice. Pauses longer than
  two seconds get cut out and wasted.
- **Look into the lens.**
- **Do not say anything private.** The words become captions in your dataset.

## Check before you move on

Watch a minute of it. Is your whole upper body in frame the whole time? Is the backdrop plain behind every part of you?
Can you hear yourself clearly? If not, record again now; fixing it later is not possible.

Then copy the video files into `my-clone/footage/`. Next: `docs/02-make-the-dataset.md`
